Common Questions

Can you customize a plan for my plot?
Yes. Our team can adjust dimensions, rooms, and materials to match your land size. Share your plot details and we’ll advise.
What do I receive when I buy?
You receive a digital package (PDF) with floor plans, elevations, and sections ready for local engineers.
Is ZenHouse only for Dar es Salaam?
No. We serve clients all over Tanzania and East Africa. We design for you remotely based on your land info.
