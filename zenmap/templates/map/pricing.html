Frequently Asked Questions

Are these prices final?
The prices listed are for the architectural drawing packages. Structural Engineering drawings or specialized BOQs are add-on services quoted separately.
Can I customize a plan I buy?
Absolutely. Minor changes are often free. Major structural changes or room additions attract a customization fee.
How do I pay?
We accept Mobile Money (M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money) and Bank Transfers. You typically pay a deposit to start customization.
What file formats do I get?
You will receive high-resolution PDF files ready for printing. CAD (DWG) files are available on Premium tiers.
